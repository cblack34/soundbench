# Recording protocol

Use one fixed setup for a comparison set:

- mono PCM WAV when possible, 48 kHz/24-bit preferred;
- the same guitar, chord, pick or fingers, microphone position, room, and gain;
- a headphone click with no audible bleed;
- no compression, EQ, normalization, limiting, reverb, noise reduction, or
  automatic gain control; and
- roughly two seconds of silence at the beginning and end.

Avoid clipping. Peaks around -12 to -6 dBFS leave practical transient headroom.
Record setup notes alongside the local audio.

For the initial calibration set, hold one E-major voicing throughout and record:

1. four isolated plucks of each fretted/open chord string, low to high;
2. eight isolated medium downstrokes with about three seconds between attacks;
3. four bars of even quarter-note downstrokes at 60 BPM; and
4. four bars with beats 1 and 3 emphasized by relaxing beats 2 and 4.

Equivalent isolated, even, and accented upstroke takes may be captured without
moving the setup. Record mixed patterns only after the one-direction references
are understood.
